You say it in one sentence. A lead plans it, builders write it in their own containers, a reviewer sends back what is not good enough, and you ship it from one card.

The majhi stands at the back of the boat with the long oar. He does not row. He reads the water and keeps the boat pointed at the ghat. With majhi, that is your job. The captain agent handles the rest when you are away, inside the budget you set.

The desk

Every client's tasks on one board, with each agent's state, the plan and every file change, live.

One boat per client

Each workspace sails alone. Nothing crosses from one to another.

  • Its own keys and loginsAI accounts, git identity, tokens and memory belong to one workspace. Agents never touch your SSH keys.
  • Small calls cost nothingLaya, a small open model on your machine, answers the many quick questions. Big models get the real work.
  • It keeps rowingLaptop asleep, Wi-Fi gone, usage limit hit: agents pause and pick up where they left off.
