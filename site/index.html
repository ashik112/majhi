Get majhi Source

You say it in one sentence. A lead plans it, builders write it in their own containers, a reviewer sends back what is not good enough, and you ship it from one card.

It runs the desk

It sails through the night

Turn on autonomous mode and the captain works your backlog like you would: it picks tasks by priority and due date, forms crews, ships finished work and leaves you a summary in the morning. It stays inside the budget you set for each client.

It does not sink

Laptop asleep, Wi-Fi gone, a usage limit hit, a model refusing a request. Agents pause and pick up where they stopped, a blocked step goes to a teammate, and unshipped work is counted before anything can close.

It ships, not just writes

Merge, squash or rebase into any branch, push, or open pull and merge requests on GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket. Several repos ship together or not at all. A conflict is one click.

It only calls you when it matters

One bell for approvals, questions and blocked work. You choose what needs your click, client by client, from careful to hands-off.

It costs less every day

Laya, a small open model running free on your machine, makes the hundreds of small calls. The big models only get the work that needs them, and every account's usage and cost is on screen.

It learns your code

Lasting facts from every task are kept per repo and brought back next time, so the crew stops relearning the same quirks.

Inside majhi

One boat per client

  • Its own accounts and loginsEach client's Claude and Codex accounts, git identity and tokens stay with that client.
  • Its own repos and secretsAgents work in containers that see one task. They never see your SSH keys or another client's code.
  • Its own costs and memorySpend, limits and what the crew has learned are counted and kept per client.
