You steer.The agents row.

Run AI coding agents for every client from one place, each client in its own boat. They do the work. You decide what ships.

Get majhi Source

Every client gets its own crew, its own accounts and its own rules. Nothing crosses between them. Bring the subscriptions you already pay for, and the captain runs it all from one desk on your machine.

Why the boat keeps moving

It rows through the night

Switch on autonomous mode before bed. The captain takes tasks by priority and due date, picks a crew for each one, ships what passes review and leaves you a summary for the morning. Each client has a spend cap it won't cross.

It doesn't capsize

Laptop asleep, Wi-Fi down, a usage limit hit, a model refusing a request. The run stops at its last checkpoint and picks up from there, or hands the step to another agent. No agent can close a task while its work sits unshipped. Only you can.

It reaches the other bank

Merge, squash or rebase into any branch. Push, or open pull and merge requests on GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket. A change across three repos lands in all three or in none. A conflict opens in one click.

It calls out only when it needs you

Approvals, questions and stuck work land in one bell. Client by client, you choose which steps wait for your click and which go ahead on their own.

It rows cheap

Laya, a small open model running free on your machine, makes the hundreds of small calls, like routing tasks and picking models. Claude and Codex only get the real work, and every account's usage and cost is on screen.

It remembers the river

Every finished task leaves notes on the repo it touched. The next crew reads them first, so nobody relearns the same quirks.

Inside majhi

One boat per client

Acme's agents can't read Globex's repos, keys or notes. The walls are built into the containers, not a setting you have to remember.

  • Its own oarsAcme's Claude and Codex logins, host tokens and git identity belong to Acme. Commits for Acme go out under the name and email you set for Acme.
  • Its own hullEach agent runs in a container that holds one task's worktrees and nothing else. It never sees your SSH keys, majhi's own config or another client's code.
  • Its own ledgerSpend, usage limits, budgets and what the crew has learned are kept per client. Globex's notes never show up in an Acme task.

Come aboard

You need Docker and one command. A short trip down the river then sets up your folders, AI accounts, clients and git logins.
