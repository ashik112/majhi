# The captain: design note (workflow reset, step 5)

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-10-20 with the answers in section 6. Built on branch `feat/captain`. Read `docs/briefs/workflow-reset.md` (rules for every step, step 5, "Autonomous: one switch", priority and preemption, step 1b, step 3 console), `docs/briefs/quality-bar.md`, `docs/briefs/test-guarantees.md` and `docs/briefs/incidents.md` (its patterns are reused) first. Every sample name below is generic (Acme, Globex, Northwind).

Goal: one captain the owner talks to, answering about every workspace, running on one account unless a contract says otherwise; one Autonomous switch with an end time and rules the owner never has to manage; a captain that starts every turn knowing the owner's intent and the workspace's wiki, with only the tools that place needs; a morning brief per workspace and a weekly client report. Most parts exist in a form that is per workspace, per setting or per page. This note says which, and what to delete.

## 2 Design

### 2.1 One conversation, one account

- **The conversation is a read, not a migration.** The owner sees one chat, the root "Captain" chat. `roomWithCaptain` for it merges the root's own lines with the untagged lines of every `captain_lanes` chat (both jobs) and tags each line with its workspace from the lane map. The top bar's workspace filter filters those lines by workspace id. Zero rows are copied or rewritten; old "Captain: Acme" history stays where it is and is found by the existing room search (`room_search` with the `t.org` filter) and the palette's workspace filter. `store/conversations.ts LISTED` hides `CAPTAIN_LANE_BRIEF` the way it hides the reacting lane; `buildChips` drops `kind: "lane"`.
- **Lane chats become steps.** A step is a turn in a workspace's lane chat. The task keeps that workspace's org, so the run gets that workspace's git, SSH, connections, mounts and wiki and nothing else (guarantees 8 to 13, kept). The lane keeps its own session memory, which is how a step remembers its workspace. Nothing about a step is shown as a row; its words appear in the one conversation, tagged.
- **Who answers (owner, 2026-10-20: both).** With a workspace picked in the top bar, the captain works scoped to it: the owner's message goes to that workspace's step. With All picked, the owner owns all of it: the desk may read full details across workspaces in the owner's own All conversation and may act on any workspace. What stays isolated is the step: every action, tool call and agent run happens in one workspace through the typed call `captain.step({ org, ask })`, sent to that workspace's lane through the existing `Lanes.tell`, with only that workspace's version control, SSH, connections, wiki and account. Nothing from workspace A goes into a step, prompt, task brief or outbound message for workspace B, and client-facing text is built only from that client's own workspace. The step never receives the desk's transcript. The root chat keeps its read-only mounts for the owner's All conversation (reads, not steps).
- **One account.** `Lanes.account(org)` flips its default: the captain agent's account (`frontmatter.account`) runs the desk and every step. `autonomy.orgs.<org>.account`, relabelled "Captain uses this workspace's account", replaces it for that workspace, and must belong to that workspace (the `withAccount` rule stays, test 14 stays). The old preference for the workspace's own account and the `noAccount` problem go.
- **Fallback.** The captain agent's existing `fallback` field names the second agent and so the second account; `Lanes` uses it when `rest()` reports the main account at its limit or signed out, with one line in the conversation ("Captain is on the fallback account until 14:00"). It reuses `resume.handoff` and the account state that already exist; no new mechanism.
- **Cannot run.** `Lanes.account` returns a typed `CannotRun` reason: `signed-out | at-limit | provider-not-allowed | account-of-another-workspace | no-account`, with the reset time when it is a limit. The decision `captainPayDecisionId` becomes `captainCannotRunId`: one row per workspace in Needs you (Decide), merged by a queue `MergeKey` when several workspaces share a cause. Its action is the fix named by the reason (Sign in, Pick account), never a list of Private accounts to borrow.

### 2.2 The Autonomous switch (replaces Auto-pilot)

- **State.** `AutonomyModeSchema` becomes `off | on`. `autonomy_state` gains `until` (ISO, null for "until I turn it off"), one migration. Existing `on` stays on with no end; `paused` and `stopping` already read as on and off. The existing one-minute tick turns it off when `until` has passed, writing a `mode` event "Turned off, until 8:00 ended" by `majhi`. Presets in the owner's zone: I turn it off, Until 8:00, Tonight (23:00), 2 hours. The captain cannot turn it on or move `until`: `autonomy.start` stays a proposal card (`CAPTAIN_PROPOSALS`).
- **Off.** It works only when asked: nothing is picked from the backlog. Tasks it was working finish their current step and pause at a checkpoint with one line; turning on again lists and resumes them (the existing `stopping` behaviour as the default; nothing is lost). Reading and answering never read the switch: `mayWork` takes the typed `Act` and returns ok for `read` whatever the mode (only Pause all, the old Stop everything, refuses all). "Pause all" is the brief's name for the existing `halted()`.
- **Fixed rules (no settings).**
  1. It starts only tasks the owner made or approved: `Task.idea` unset. The gate treats `captain` origin without `ownerAsked` as an idea (`captain-made`, one new `IdeaReason`), so the captain never works on its own ideas; incident and client-chat work stays reacting and never reads the switch.
  2. A task that fails twice stops and goes to Needs you. A failure is an ended run with a failing `stopReason` since the captain took the task (derived from run rows, nothing stored); the second one raises the lifecycle hold `autonomous-failed`, lifted by the owner only.
  3. At most 2 captain tasks at once across all workspaces: `limits.captain_tasks` (default 2, in the zod schema and `majhi.yaml` only) replaces per-workspace `tasksAtOnce`. In the line, priority first, then the owner's origin before the captain's, then age (the step 1 gate order).
  4. A task's budget comes from its size (Laya's rating, as `sizes.ts` rates today; unknown counts as medium): `limits.captain_task_cost` small/medium/large, defaults in the schema. Over it the task pauses and `budget-asks.ts` asks. The day's cap keeps `autonomy.day` with its default, and the account floors (`floors`, no start below 10% of a window or 5% of the week) are the headroom rule. Both stay in Settings and never need to be changed.
  5. Ships follow the workspace's authority level (`levelAllows`). A workspace at Ask me is never worked: `leftOutWhy` reads the level, not the legacy `start` row.
- **Status.** One line under the switch in the top bar, built from `autonomy.status`: "Autonomous · 2 working · $3.10 of $15 today · until 8:00". Stops show in Needs you. The control is the existing `Menu` and segmented patterns of `console/top-bar.tsx`; the switch and its menu are first added to `design/console.html` and compared pair by pair before the web commit.
- **Deleted settings.** `pick` (size rule and `sizes.ts` as a filter), `tasksAtOnce`, `hours` (replaced by `until`), per-task `noAutonomy` (a migration files marked open tasks as ideas, reason `held-by-owner`). Kept because they protect a client, not because they steer Autonomous: `freeze`, `branches`, `providers`, `holds`, `incident`, `cap`, `account`, `floors`, `day`, `instructions` (the standing intent). The startup migration rewrites `majhi.yaml` once through the config service (comments kept), the way the level migration did; until then the schema still accepts the old keys and ignores them.

### 2.3 What a turn knows, and what it can call

- **Places.** `CAPTAIN_PLACES: Record<Place, readonly CommandName[]>` in `packages/shared` for `needs`, `work`, `chat`, plus `core` (about 25 reads every turn: lists, `decisions.recommend`, `wiki.ask`, memory, usage, ask-the-owner). Typed against `CommandName`, so a removed command fails to compile, like `APPROVAL_GROUP_DEFS`. It is filled from the commands the captain actually called in the audit log over 30 days plus the tools its preambles name, not from a guess. `adminTools(place)` replaces the module-level `TOOLS`; `mcp.ts` builds the list per session from the wake's place (an item, a backlog tick, the owner's message). A command in no place is not offered to the captain; the owner reaches it in Settings or asks, and a gap goes to `majhi_captain_reportBug`. Target: at most 60 tools and at most a quarter of today's schema size per turn.
- **Context block.** One function `turnContext({ place, org })` returns a typed value rendered by one renderer into the first prompt: the standing intent (instructions with no org, plus this workspace's; never another's), the workspace's authority level and Autonomous state with `until`, and the wiki summary: the `overview` pages of the workspace's projects, cut to 1,200 characters, cached by built commit, only when `wikiEnabled(org)`. The desk (All) gets the intent of every workspace and a one-line level per workspace. `DIGEST_MAX_CHARS` stays 6,000; the wiki summary and intent count inside it, and what is cut is counted.
- **Preambles.** `ADMIN_PREAMBLE` and `AUTONOMY_PREAMBLE` are trimmed to the sections of the turn's place and updated to the level, Autonomous and steps; stale lines go.
- **Scoping by id.** In `lane-scope.ts`, `mentions()`, `textWorkspace()` and the task-id regex are deleted. A row belongs to the workspace its typed fields name; a row with none belongs to every workspace, as today for models and days. `ScopeWorld.task` returns `PRIVATE` for a task with no org, so Private scopes by id too and `forceOrg` needs no special case.

### 2.4 Morning brief per workspace and the weekly client report

- **Morning brief.** `AgendaService` gains an `org` scope: `overnight(from, to, org)` and the facts per workspace (shipped, failed, spent against its cap, what Autonomous took, finished and spent, what needs the owner). Same pattern as today: code gathers facts, the smallest model words them or the template does, titles fenced as data. It runs at `summary_at` (default 08:00) in the workspace's `tz` else the owner's, for a workspace that had activity or an Autonomous task; otherwise nothing is made and no model is called. `autonomy/summary.ts` and its event kind are deleted, `SHIPS` moves to a shared place the brief reads.
- **Where it shows.** A Done item per workspace in Needs you, group "Done by the captain": "Morning brief · Acme", with Read it (the spec's pattern for "Client told"). Read it opens the brief as the newest line of the one conversation, tagged Acme. No new screen. Once a morning it also pushes: one short combined message across workspaces (owner, 2026-10-20), through step 4's channel fan-out (desktop, ntfy, Telegram). It is not an incident ping: it is outside `pingFor`, writes no ping record and never counts toward or changes the incident ping rules.
- **Weekly client report.** Monday 08:00 in the workspace's `tz`, for the previous Monday to Sunday, for workspaces with a client room. Facts, all derived: what shipped (merged and deployed tasks, `SHIPS`), time (sum of run spans `endedAt - startedAt` per workspace), agent cost (`usage` per org), incidents and uptime (per watch, minutes failing from incident spans over minutes in the week, one pure function shared with `failingMinutes`). One internal copy (all of it) and one client copy: shipped, hours of agent work, incidents and uptime, and the agent cost only when the workspace's "Show cost to client" switch is on (off by default). Cost bound: at most one model call per workspace per week and one per day for the brief, input under 2,000 tokens, each attributed (workspace, "brief" or "report") in `usage`; nothing happened means no call; an account at its limit means the template.
- **Outward path.** The report is the `report` room item of step 4 with `kind: weekly` and a period, posted in the workspace's lane chat so it shows in the conversation. It follows the workspace's Client update switch (default Ask me): Ask me posts a "Review report" row in Decide (Edit, Send); Captain sends sends it and leaves a Done item "Client told: weekly report". Both go through the one `ChatDesk.report` path and `railsFor` with `report: true`, so the fixed rails (secret, another client's name, internal ids) hold either way. The recipient rooms are the ones with the per-chat "Gets weekly report" switch on (off by default); none on means internal only.

### 2.5 Reserved to the owner

Nothing below is reachable by the captain, a step or the desk, and no new path changes that: authority level and the Fix and Client update switches (`authority.set`); the Autonomous switch and its end (a proposal card only); the account override and fallback (`autonomy.configure` is a proposal); push, MR and merge outside the level (`levelAllows`); production deploys and client messages unless the workspace's switch says Captain ships or sends; any weekly report outside its switch; secrets, writes on client servers, deleting outside the sandbox, going over budget; removing a worktree with uncommitted changes; pausing a task the owner started unasked; answering decisions (`inbox`); instructions found in repos, attachments, client text or tracker items (always fenced data, in briefs and reports too).

## 6 Owner answers (2026-10-20)

1. Where does a message go? Both. A workspace picked in the top bar scopes the captain to it. With All, the captain reads full details across workspaces in the owner's own conversation and acts on any workspace, but every action runs as a step in one workspace with only its own version control, SSH, connections, wiki and account, and nothing of one workspace reaches a step, task brief, prompt or outbound message of another. Guarantee test 9 holds it.
2. Weekly report: no cost to the client by default; a per-workspace switch "Show cost to client", off.
3. Recipients: a per-chat switch "Gets weekly report", off by default.
4. Morning brief: it pushes, once a morning, one combined short message across workspaces, to the owner's alert channels (Telegram and ntfy) through step 4's fan-out. Not an incident ping. The full brief stays as a Done item and a chat line.

Decided in this note without a question: the `until` presets and "Tonight" at 23:00; turning off lets running tasks finish their step and pause; `limits.captain_tasks` 2; task budgets by size; wiki overview as the summary; `freeze`, `branches`, `providers` kept and `hours` dropped; the fallback named by the owner on the captain agent; the weekly report for a workspace with no client room stays internal in Chat; the old lane chats hidden, never archived or copied; Pause all is the existing Stop everything; the `majhi.yaml` migration writes atomically and keeps a backup of the old file.
